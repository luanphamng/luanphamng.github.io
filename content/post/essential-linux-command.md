## What’s inside
This infographic covers:
- **Navigation & Files**: List, create, and remove files or directories.
- **File Operations**: Copy, move, change permissions, and check disk usage.
- **Content Viewing**: View and edit files directly from the terminal.
- **Search & Filter**: Find files and search text efficiently.
- **Process Management**: Monitor and control running processes.
- **Network & System**: Check connectivity, download files, and get system info.
- **Help & Manual**: Quickly find help, manuals, and your command history.
